02About / practice & background
The designer

Looking closely,
designing deliberately.

Priyanka Vishwakarma is a fashion design fresher working across womenswear, textile and surface development, styling, illustration and design research.

Her approach is rooted in India's craft and textile heritage, reinterpreted through contemporary proposals with attention to colour, construction, texture and the visual story around a garment.

03Process / from reference to result
Studio method

Research becomes material.
Material becomes form.

OBSERVE → COLLECT → TEST → REFINE

Design development moves through cultural research, material study, colour story, mood and silhouette exploration, then into textile experimentation and garment documentation.

I

Research

Culture, craft, architecture, archival references.

II

Develop

Colour, mood, proportion and silhouette.

III

Material

Fabric, surface, embroidery and manipulation.

IV

Document

Boards, details and garment presentation.
