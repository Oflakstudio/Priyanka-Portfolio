About the designer

A practice that starts with looking closely.

Priyanka Vishwakarma is a fashion design fresher working across womenswear, textile and surface development, styling, illustration and design research.

Her work brings cultural and material references into contemporary design proposals, with an emphasis on colour, construction, textile detail and the visual story around a garment.
