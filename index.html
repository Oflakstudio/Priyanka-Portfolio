02

About

Colours, textiles and silhouettes with a precise eye for detail.

Priyanka Vishwakarma is an emerging fashion designer from Chhattisgarh, India, trained across professional fashion designing and handloom & textile technology.

Her practice moves between womenswear design, fashion illustration, textile and surface development, styling, colour stories, mood boards, fabric selection and garment detailing.

She is interested in contemporary fashion that carries cultural memory without becoming nostalgic — using craft, textile and material as active design tools.
