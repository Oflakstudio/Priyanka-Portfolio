02About the designer

A considered beginning

Looking closely,
designing deliberately.

Priyanka Vishwakarma is a Fashion Design fresher from Chhattisgarh, India, with a Professional Fashion Designing diploma from Skill in a Box, Delhi, and a Diploma in Handloom and Textile Technology from IIHT.

Her practice spans womenswear design, fashion illustration, textile and surface development, styling, colour stories, moodboards, fabric selection, garment construction fundamentals and detail-led presentation.

She is seeking an entry-level opportunity across fashion design, styling, textile or garment design.
