02 — About

Colours, textiles and silhouettes
— with a keen eye for detail.

Priyanka Vishwakarma is an emerging fashion designer from Chhattisgarh, India, with a Professional Fashion Designing diploma from Skill in a Box, Delhi, and a Diploma in Handloom and Textile Technology from IIHT, Chhattisgarh.

Her practice spans womenswear design, fashion illustration, textile and surface development, styling, colour story development, moodboard development, fabric selection, and garment detailing.

Her portfolio reflects a design sensibility rooted in India's craft and textile heritage, recontextualized through contemporary womenswear.

03 — Research & ProcessDesign Development

From cultural research to material experimentation — a documented design intelligence.

Design Methodology

Research begins with cultural immersion — heritage, craft, architecture, spirituality — before moving into material study, colour story development, moodboard construction, silhouette exploration, and garment documentation. Each project is a sustained inquiry before an answer.

Research

Cultural inquiry, heritage study, archival and material sourcing

Development

Sketching, colour story, moodboard and silhouette exploration

Material

Fabric selection, surface technique, embroidery, textile work

Documentation

Front/side/back views, detail imagery, garment presentation

Creative ArchiveAdditional Creative Work

Fabric Painting

Mixed media on canvas — bold colour blocking and fine detail, drawing on traditional Indian cultural motifs.

Beadwork Embroidery

Hand embroidery with beads and threads; intricate surface decoration with zari and brocade detailing.

Patchwork

Layering and fabric appliqué — assembling textile fragments into structured surface compositions.

Accessories Research

Traditional and western styling boards documenting accessory language across heritage and contemporary fashion.
